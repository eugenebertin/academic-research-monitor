The research focus is the exploration and demonstration of novel inorganic thin films semiconductor and dielectric materials, never fabricated or only fabricated as powders. Even though the main endeavour is the study of the materials themselves, the sought after properties are mainly for applications such as solar cell absorber, transparent conductors and photonics. In the broader sense, prediction of interesting properties for quantum computing/ communication applications or  thermoelectrics are also welcome. Generally, any suggestion of optoelectronic material with breakthrough potential is welcome. The main objective of the agent will not necessarily be to select advances in the field of computational discovery, but rather to guide experimentalists and suggest interesting materials to grow.
